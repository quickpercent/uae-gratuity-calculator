Understanding UAE End of Service Gratuity (EOSB)

In the United Arab Emirates, End of Service Benefit (EOSB) or gratuity is a statutory monetary compensation provided to employees upon completing their employment contract under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021.

UAE Gratuity Calculation Formula

Gratuity is computed strictly on the employee's Basic Salary (excluding allowances such as transport, housing, and utilities). The formula operates as follows:

Service Duration Entitlement Rate per Year
Less than 1 Year 0 Days (No gratuity entitlement)
1 Year to 5 Years 21 Days' Basic Salary per Year
More than 5 Years 30 Days' Basic Salary for each additional Year

Key Provisions & Rules (MOHRE Guidelines)

  • Maximum Limit: The total accumulated gratuity payout cannot exceed two years' (24 months) worth of basic salary.
  • Pro-Rata Calculation: Fractions of a year are calculated proportionally provided the employee has completed at least 1 full year of continuous service.
  • Unpaid Leaves: Any days taken as unpaid leave are excluded from the total duration of service calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

According to UAE labor laws, if an employee resigns or is terminated before completing one continuous year of service, they are not entitled to any end-of-service gratuity pay.

Gratuity is calculated exclusively on your Basic Salary. Other components such as housing allowance, transport allowance, bonuses, or overtime payments are strictly excluded from the calculation.

The total end of service benefit payable to an employee cannot exceed two years' (24 months) total basic salary regardless of how many years they have served.
