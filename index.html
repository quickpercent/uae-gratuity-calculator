How to Calculate Gratuity in UAE

Our Dubai Gratuity Calculator follows the basic structure of UAE end of service gratuity calculation under Article 51.

1. Use Basic Salary

UAE gratuity is calculated using the applicable last basic wage. Housing, transport and other allowances are generally not included in the basic gratuity calculation.

2. Calculate Service

Enter your employment start date and last working date. Unpaid absence days can be deducted from the service period.

3. Apply UAE Formula

The standard formula uses 21 days per year for the first five years and 30 days per year for service beyond five years.

UAE Gratuity Formula

Understanding the 21 days gratuity UAE and 30 days gratuity UAE formula.

First 5 Years

Daily Basic Wage × 21 × Years

For eligible service during the first five years, the standard gratuity rate is 21 days of basic wage per year.

After 5 Years

Daily Basic Wage × 30 × Years

For eligible service beyond five years, the standard gratuity rate is 30 days of basic wage per year.

UAE Gratuity Rules 2026

Key UAE Labour Law gratuity rules relevant to end of service benefits.

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, Article 51 provides the end of service benefit rules for eligible foreign workers under the applicable full-time work pattern.

  • Eligibility generally requires at least one year of continuous service.
  • The calculation uses the applicable last basic wage.
  • The first five years use 21 days of basic wage per year.
  • Additional service after five years uses 30 days per year.
  • Unpaid absence days are excluded from the gratuity calculation.
  • Fractions of a year can be calculated proportionately after the required one-year service condition.
  • The total gratuity is subject to the applicable maximum of two years' wage.

Article 51 UAE Labour Law – Gratuity

UAE gratuity Article 51 is the key legal reference for the standard end of service gratuity calculation.

Article 51 of UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 addresses the end-of-service benefit for eligible foreign workers.

The benefit is calculated according to the last basic wage. The standard rate is 21 days for each year of the first five years and 30 days for each additional year.

The law also provides for proportional calculation for parts of a year where the applicable one-year continuous service condition has been satisfied.

The total end-of-service gratuity is subject to the applicable two-year wage maximum.

Article 53 UAE Labour Law – Payment

When is UAE gratuity paid and when should an employer pay gratuity?

Article 53 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 states that the employer shall pay the worker wages and other applicable entitlements within 14 days from the date of the end of the contract.

Therefore, the phrase UAE gratuity payment within 14 days refers to the payment requirement stated in Article 53 for applicable wages and entitlements.

Free Zones and Special Employment Regimes

Important scope information before relying on a gratuity estimate.

Not every employment relationship in the UAE should automatically be treated as identical. Some special jurisdictions and employment categories may have their own employment regulations.

  • DIFC employment can be subject to DIFC Employment Law.
  • ADGM employment can be subject to ADGM employment regulations.
  • Some free-zone employment arrangements can have special rules.
  • UAE nationals may have different pension or end-of-service arrangements.
  • Domestic workers and other special categories may have separate rules.
If your employment falls under a special legal regime, verify the applicable rules before treating this estimate as your final settlement amount.
