UAE Gratuity Calculator for Dubai and the UAE

The UAE Gratuity Calculator helps employees estimate their end of service gratuity (EOSB) based on their last basic monthly salary and length of continuous service. It can be used by eligible private-sector employees in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain.

For eligible full-time foreign workers, Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 provides the basic framework for calculating end of service benefits.

Important: The calculator uses the last basic wage for the gratuity calculation. Allowances and other components of total compensation are not used as the gratuity wage under Article 51.

How Is UAE Gratuity Calculated?

For an eligible full-time foreign worker who has completed at least one continuous year, the gratuity calculation is based on the last basic wage.

Service Period Gratuity Rate
Less than 1 year No Article 51 gratuity entitlement
First 5 years 21 days of basic wage per year
Years exceeding 5 years 30 days of basic wage per year
Maximum Not more than 2 years' wage

UAE Gratuity Formula

Daily Basic Wage

Daily Basic Wage = Basic Monthly Salary ÷ 30

First Five Years

Gratuity = Daily Basic Wage × 21 × Years of Service

After Five Years

The first five years are calculated at 21 days per year. The period exceeding five years is calculated at 30 days per year.

The calculator automatically separates these two portions and displays the amount for each part.

UAE End of Service Benefits

End of service benefits are payments that may become due when an employment relationship ends. The exact entitlement can depend on the employee's work pattern, employment regime and applicable law.

This calculator focuses on the Article 51 framework for eligible full-time foreign workers. Employees working under special regimes or different work patterns should verify the rules applicable to their employment.

Why Basic Salary Matters

One of the most important points when calculating UAE gratuity is the difference between basic salary and total salary.

For the Article 51 calculation, gratuity is calculated using the employee's last basic wage. Therefore, if your monthly package is AED 12,000 but your basic salary is AED 7,000, the gratuity calculation should use the applicable basic wage rather than simply using AED 12,000 as the gratuity salary.

Unpaid Leave and Gratuity

Article 51 states that unpaid days of absence are not included in the service term used for end of service benefits.

For this reason, the calculator includes an optional Unpaid Leave / Unpaid Absence Days field. If you have no unpaid days, simply leave it at zero.

UAE Labour Law Article 51

Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 covers end of service benefits for full-time workers. For eligible full-time foreign workers, the law specifies:

  • 21 days of wage for each year of the first five years.
  • 30 days of wage for each year exceeding five years.
  • Pro-rata benefits for parts of a year after completing one continuous year.
  • Unpaid days of absence are not included in the service term.
  • The calculation is based on the last basic wage.
  • The total benefit is subject to a maximum of two years' wage.
