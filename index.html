How UAE End-of-Service Gratuity is Calculated

According to UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, workers completing at least one full year of continuous service are entitled to end-of-service gratuity:

  • 1 to 5 Years of Service: 21 days' basic salary for each year of service.
  • More than 5 Years of Service: 30 days' basic salary for each additional year beyond 5 years.
  • Maximum Limit: Total gratuity cannot exceed 2 years' total salary.
