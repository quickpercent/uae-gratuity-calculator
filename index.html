UAE Gratuity Formula: 21 Days and 30 Days

Under the standard Article 51 formula for eligible full-time foreign workers, gratuity is calculated using the last basic wage.

First 5 years: 21 days of basic wage for each year.

After 5 years: 30 days of basic wage for each additional year.
Service Period Gratuity Rate Example Days
Less than 1 year Standard Article 51 gratuity not earned 0 days

Worked UAE Gratuity Calculation Example

Suppose an employee has a basic monthly salary of AED 10,000 and completes exactly 5 years of eligible service.

Daily basic wage = AED 10,000 ÷ 30 = AED 333.33

Gratuity days = 5 × 21 = 105 days

Estimated gratuity = AED 333.33 × 105 = AED 35,000

For a service period above five years, the first five years remain at 21 days per year and each additional year uses the 30-day rate, subject to the applicable maximum cap.

UAE Gratuity Rules and End of Service Benefits

The standard UAE federal private-sector gratuity framework provides end of service benefits for eligible full-time foreign workers who complete at least one year of continuous service.

  • Gratuity is calculated using the last basic wage.
  • The first five years use 21 days of basic wage per year.
  • Additional years after five use 30 days per year.
  • Parts of a year can be calculated proportionally after the one-year continuous-service requirement is met.
  • Unpaid days of absence are excluded from the service term.
  • The total benefit cannot exceed two years' wage under Article 51.

Article 51 – UAE End of Service Gratuity

Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 sets out the end of service benefits for full-time workers. For eligible foreign workers who complete one year or more of continuous service, the calculation is based on the basic wage.

Article 51 key points:

21 days of basic wage for every year of the first five years.

30 days of basic wage for every year exceeding five years.

Parts of a year are calculated proportionally after one year of continuous service.

Unpaid absence days are not included in the service term.

Total end of service benefits cannot exceed two years' wage.

Article 53 – When Should UAE End of Service Benefits Be Paid?

Article 53 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 states that the employer shall pay the worker's wages and other applicable entitlements within 14 days from the end date of the contract term.

14-Day Rule: Article 53 provides a 14-day period from the contract end date for payment of wages and applicable entitlements.

If you have a dispute regarding your employment entitlements, seek appropriate guidance from the relevant UAE authority or qualified professional.

Free Zones, DIFC and ADGM: Important Note

Not every employee working in Dubai or another UAE emirate should automatically use the standard federal gratuity formula. Some jurisdictions and employment categories have separate rules or regulations.

DIFC

Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) has its own employment framework. Employees working under DIFC employment rules should verify the applicable DIFC provisions.

ADGM

Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) has its own employment regulations. ADGM employees should use the applicable ADGM rules rather than automatically relying on this federal estimate.

Other Free Zones

Free-zone employment arrangements can have specific regulatory considerations. Always confirm which employment law applies to your contract before relying on an estimate.
